# Contact and human help

## Talking to a person
You can ask for a person at any time. The assistant hands the conversation over with a short summary, so you do not need to repeat yourself.

## What the assistant cannot help with
The assistant answers questions about our policies, parts, fitment, prices, stock and your own orders. It cannot give technical repair or installation advice, diagnose faults, discuss other companies, or help with anything unrelated to Halden Moto Parts.

## Support hours
Support replies on working days, usually within one working day. Urgent safety concerns about a part you bought should be sent by email with the word SAFETY in the subject line: support@haldenmoto.example.
