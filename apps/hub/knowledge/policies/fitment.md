# Fitment and My garage

## How fitment works
Add your bike under My garage (year, make and model). Every part then shows one of three labels for your bike: Fits, Does not fit, or Check fitment.

## What Check fitment means
Check fitment means we have no fitment data for that part. It does not mean the part fits. Please confirm with a person before ordering a part marked Check fitment, especially a safety part.

## Bikes we cover
We hold fitment data for the Honda CB500F (2013 to 2015, 2016 to 2018 and 2019 to 2021), Yamaha MT-07 (2014 to 2020 and 2021 to 2024), Kawasaki Z650 (2017 to 2023), Triumph Street Triple 765 (2017 to 2022), Suzuki SV650 (2016 to 2024) and Ducati Monster 821 (2018 to 2020).

## Model years matter
Some parts differ between model years. For example, the 42 tooth rear sprocket for the CB500F is a different part for 2013 to 2015 bikes than for 2016 and later bikes, because the offset differs.

## ABS and other variants
Our fitment table lists model generations by year. It does not list ABS or non-ABS variants separately, so for variant-specific questions we hand you to a person.

## Wrong fitment guarantee
If a part marked Fits for your bike turns out not to fit, we collect it and refund it in full, including return shipping.
