# Warranty

## Coverage
Mechanical and electrical parts carry a 12 month warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date.

## What is not covered
Normal wear is not covered. Brake pads, discs, chains, sprockets, tyres, spark plugs and filters wear out with use. Damage from incorrect installation, accidents, or fitting a part that does not match your bike is not covered either.

## How to claim
Contact support with your order number, the email used at checkout, and photos of the part and the fault. If the claim is accepted we replace the part or refund it.

## Safety
If you think a defective part is a safety risk, stop riding the bike and contact us right away.
