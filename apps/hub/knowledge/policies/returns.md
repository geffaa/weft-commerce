# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return a part within 30 days of delivery. The part must be unused, uninstalled and in its original packaging. The window is the same in the UK, EU, US and Australia.

## Wrong part or wrong fitment
If you ordered a part that does not fit your bike, we accept the return as long as the part was never installed. Check "My garage" before ordering: each part shows Fits, Does not fit or Check fitment for your bike.

## Safety parts
Brake pads, brake discs and tyres cannot be returned once they have been fitted or the packaging has been opened, because we cannot resell them safely.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days after we receive the part. If there is no original payment on file, we issue store credit instead.

## Who pays for return shipping
You pay for return shipping, unless the part is faulty or we sent the wrong item. In that case we pay, and we send a prepaid label.

## How to start a return
Open your order status page and choose Start a return, or contact support with your order number and the email used at checkout.
