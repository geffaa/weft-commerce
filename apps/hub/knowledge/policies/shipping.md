# Shipping and delivery

## UK delivery
Standard delivery in the UK is tracked and takes 2 to 4 working days. Next working day delivery is available if you order before 14:00. Delivery within the UK is free for orders over 60 pounds.

## Europe, United States and Australia
We ship to the UK, selected EU countries (Germany, France, Spain, Italy and the Netherlands), the United States and Australia. Standard delivery to the EU takes 3 to 6 working days, to the US 5 to 9 working days. Delivery to Australia is tracked; the checkout shows the delivery time before you pay.

## Delivery prices
UK: standard delivery costs 4.95 pounds, next working day costs 9.95 pounds. Europe: standard 9.50 euros, express 19 euros. United States: standard 12 dollars, express 24 dollars. Australia: standard 16 Australian dollars, express 32 Australian dollars. The checkout always shows the exact price for your address before you pay.

## Where parts ship from
Orders ship from our warehouse in Leeds, UK. Orders placed before 15:00 on a working day usually ship the same day when the part is in stock.

## Duties and taxes
UK prices include 20 percent VAT. Australian prices include 10 percent GST. For EU and US orders, duties and taxes where they apply are shown at checkout before you pay.

## Tracking
You receive a tracking link by email once your order has shipped. The order status page shows the same progress.

## Countries we do not ship to
We do not currently ship anywhere outside the UK, the five EU countries listed above, the United States and Australia. For other countries, please contact support.
