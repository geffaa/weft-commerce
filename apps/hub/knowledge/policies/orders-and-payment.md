# Orders and payment

## Paying
You pay by debit or credit card. If your bank asks for extra confirmation (3-D Secure), you will see a verification step. Your order is only created after the payment is confirmed.

## Order status
Your order status page shows these steps: order placed, payment confirmed, packing, shipped and delivered.

## Changing or cancelling an order
You can change or cancel an order until it moves to packing. After that, contact support, and we will do our best to stop it before it ships.

## Looking up an order
To look up an order we need the order number and the email address used at checkout. We only share the order status, the number of items and the total, never the delivery address.

## Trade accounts
Approved trade customers can pay on account with net 30 terms. Please contact support to apply.

## Prices
Prices and stock levels change. The assistant always reads the live price and stock for the region you are browsing; it never quotes them from memory.
